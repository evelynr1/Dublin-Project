The Peace Park

Park Description

Peace Park is a small park with ample bench seating across the street from Christchurch Cathedral. The park sits a few feet below street level which contributes to its peaceful atmosphere by reducing the sounds of the bustling street.

Park Information

Address: 13 Christchurch Pl, Wood Quay, Dublin, Ireland

Hours: 10AM to 8PM daily

Website: dublincity.ie/parks-and-nature

Amenities:

  • Seating
  • Shaded areas

Accessibility

This park is wheelchair accessible. There is a ramp and stairs at the park entrance. There are five steps up to the tree artwork in the back of the park to view it up close, but it is visible thorughout the whole park.
