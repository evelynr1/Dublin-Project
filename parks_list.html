Parks in Dublin

Parks List

Dubh Linn

Perfect for history lovers, Dubh Linn Gardens is located behind the Dublin Castle and features two museums, the Garda Memorial Garden, and a circular lawns decorated with patterns representing sea serpents.

Iveagh Gardens

Featuring a yew maze, rose garden, and even a waterfall, Iveagh Gardens is the perfect getaway in the hustle of the city center. The archery field which is sunk down below street level, is a great place to take in the sun while serenely lounging in the grass or chatting with friends.

The Peace Park

Peace Park is a small park with ample bench seating across the street from Christchurch Cathedral. The park sits a few feet below street level which contributes to its peaceful atmosphere by reducing the sounds of the bustling street.

St Patrick's Park

With a weekly Sunday market, it's very own cafe, and the occassional tolling of church bells, this park is a great place to people watch. Or dog watch if you prefer, as there are frequently dog owners playing fetch in the grassy center of the park.

St Stephen's Green

Comprised of 22 acres of greenspace in Dublin city center, St. Stephen's Green is the largest of the city's Georgian Garden Squares. The park also features 21 historical placards to read as you stroll through. Additionally, it has a playground, ample seating, and open green spaces making it perfect for a stroll, picnic, or playdate!
