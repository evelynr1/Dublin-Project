Accessibility Means Having Options

As I learned during a group discussion at the Irish Wheelchair Association Headquarters and from a talk by Haben Girma at aXe Con 2026, making things accessible means giving people options.

Accessibility is not a one size fits all approach. As such, I want this website to be adaptable to various needs.

Things you can customize on this site

  • Text size
  • Color or black-and-white for highest contrast
  • No images
