St Stephen's Green

Park Description

Comprised of 22 acres of greenspace in Dublin city center, St. Stephen's Green is the largest of the city's Georgian Garden Squares. The park also features 21 historical placards to read as you stroll through. Additionally, it has a playground, ample seating, and open green spaces making it perfect for a stroll, picnic, or playdate!

Accessibility

St Stephen's Green is mostly wheelchair accessible. Every entrance is wheelchair accessible and the paths are wide and paved. There is a seating area by the W. B. Yeats memorial which is only accesible by going up steps. There is a public bathroom for 0.25 euros available at St Stephen's Green Shopping Centre just outside the park.
