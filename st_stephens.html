St Stephen's Green

Park Description

Peac

Park Information

Address: St Stephen's Green, Dublin 2, Ireland

Hours: 7:30AM to 8PM daily

How to get there: walking, bus, tram

Amenities

  • Clear signage
  • Green spaces
  • Playground
  • Seating
  • Shaded areas
  • Walking paths
  • Waste disposal
  • Water features

Accessibility

This park is mostly wheelchair accessible. Every entrance is wheelchair accessible and the paths are wide and paved. There is a seating area by the W. B. Yeats memorial which is only accesible by going up steps. There is a public bathroom for 0.25 euros available at St Stephen's Green Shopping Centre just outside the park.
