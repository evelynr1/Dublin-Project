St Patrick's Park

Park Description

With a weekly Sunday market, it's very own cafe, and the occassional tolling of church bells, this park is a great place to people watch. Or dog watch if you prefer, as there are frequently dog owners playing fetch in the grassy center of the park.

Park Information

Address: Bully Alley St, Dublin, Ireland

Hours: 7:30AM to 9PM daily

How to get there: walking, bus

Amenities

  • Seating
  • Shaded areas

  • Waste disposal

Accessibility

This park is wheelchair accessible.
