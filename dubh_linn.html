Dubh Linn Gardens

Park Description

Perfect for history lovers, Dubh Linn Gardens is located behind the Dublin Castle and features two museums, the Garda Memorial Garden, and a circular lawns decorated with patterns representing sea serpents.

Park Information

Address: Dublin Castle, Dame St, Dublin, Ireland

Hours: 6:30AM-7PM daily

Website: The Castle Gardens | Dublin Castle

How to get there: walking, bus

Amenities

  • Gardens

  • Shaded areas

  • Waste disposal

Accessibility

This park is wheelchair accessible with the exception of the overlook platform which is only accessible via stairs. However, both the Chester Beatty and the 1926 Census Museum have step-free alternatives. The Chester Beatty has a step-free entrance and an elevator inside. The 1926 Census Museum is one story and has a wheelchair lift to bring you up the stairs at the entrance.
