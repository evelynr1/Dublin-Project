Iveagh Gardens

Park Description

Featuring a yew maze, rose garden, and even a waterfall, Iveagh Gardens is the perfect getaway in the hustle of the city center. The archery field which is sunk down below street level, is a great place to take in the sun while serenely lounging in the grass or chatting with friends.

Park Information

Hours: 8AM to 7:30PM Monday through Saturday. 10AM to 7:30PM on Sunday.

Address: St Stephen's Green, Park, Dublin 2, D02 HX65, Ireland

Website: Iveagh Gardens | Experience a sense of wonder in Dublin’s ‘secret garden’

Accessibility: This park is wheelchair accessible.
